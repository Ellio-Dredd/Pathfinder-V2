Abstract of the project

The proposed application is to develop a "Context-Aware Route Optimizing and assistance system " delivered via an intelligent web application. The standard mapping applications do not support to critical, real-world constraints, such as arriving at a business place before it closes or meeting a fixed appointment. This application will solve this by calculating the best optimal route that includes multiple destinations and will address the problem of wasted time and frustration that are associated with poor manual planning. Unlike standard navigation apps, this system’s uniqueness lies in its multi-faceted constraint driven approach. For daily errands and tourist planning which helps tourist to find the most attractive tourist destinations and plan the trip along with it by finding the best route that covers all those destinations. It automatically determines the best possible sequence by solving a complex version of Traveling Salesperson Problem with Time Windows. Which means this application will actively considers rules like business hours, fixed appointments and task dependencies. For drivers needing immediate help, it offers intelligent roadside assistance by dynamically finding and ranking nearby service providers based on location, status and rating. This system will be developed as a modern web application with the use of custom-built AI optimization algorithm and third-party APIs which will be helpful in gathering real time and context-awareness data for algorithm’s decision making. The proposed application will be beneficial for boosting productivity for daily planners, maximizing experience for tourist and provide rapid and reliable options for drivers in distress. This proposed application will ensure a simplified, intelligent approach to both complex planning and real-time assistance.

Description of your artefact
Travelling is an important aspect of the people’s lives, for tourism, livelihood or personal purposes. In the present people often rely on google maps (GoogleMaps, 2023) and other solutions such as petal maps,( petal maps,2020) apple maps or other third-party solutions. The problem encountered was the lack of solution to find the best & optimal route that consist of all the given destinations. In this proposed project “Pathfinder”, users can enter the multiple destinations they want to go to, then the “Pathfinder” will automatically plan the journey by considering the shortest distance and shortest time spent form the stating point to all the destinations and also succeeding the critical, real-world time constraints, minimizing the hassle for users to plan the journey manually, saving time and cost for the journey. This solution is consist of two modes as “Daily Planer mode” and “Tourism mode”, “Daily Planer mode” is focused on daily activities while “Tourism mode” is focused on vacation trips with required options. (Algorithms for Context-Awareness Route Generation, 2021)

Aim – The aim of this project is to develop a context-aware Web Application System that provides optimised route for multiple daily errands and tourist planning alongside intelligent roadside assistance.
Objectives –
To research and design a system architecture for solving the Traveling Salesman Problem with Time window constraints and dynamic, real-time service finding.
To implement AI optimization engines, capable of finding the a near optimal routes in the daily errand and tourist planning modes.
To implement ranking algorithm for the roadside assistant feature prioritizing service providers based on available distance, operational status and user ratings  
To integrate third party Api’s to fetch and supply the optimization engine and all application modes with real-world data.
To test and validate the system’s effectiveness and user friendliness to ensure the daily planner, Tourist itinerary and Roadside assistance features are feasible and efficient
